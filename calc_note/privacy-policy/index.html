CalcNote

Privacy Policy

CalcNote is a notepad-style calculator. Your notes and calculations stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Last updated: March 10, 2026

Summary

  • No account required.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No collection of personal data.
  • Files you open or save stay on your device.

Information We Collect

CalcNote does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data You Create

Notes, calculations, and variables you enter are processed locally on your device. If you choose to save or export files, those files are stored where you select on your device.

Permissions

CalcNote may request access to your device storage when you open or save files. This is only used to read or write the files you select.

Third-Party Services

CalcNote does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

CalcNote is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
